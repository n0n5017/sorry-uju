Obianuju

This is for you.

I said things to you
that I should never have said.

I called you a whore.

I said I would never marry a whore.

I said my children should not see your nudes.

I mocked your past.

I used your body and your history as weapons when I was angry.

There is no excuse for that.

Being hurt does not give me the right to speak to you that way.

I was angry.

And instead of protecting what we have,

I chose to tear you down with the sharpest words I could find.

That is on me.

You did not deserve those words.

Not then.
Not ever.

This is the second time we have broken up.

The sixth time we almost did.

I do not want to keep doing this to you.

I am sorry, Uju.

Really sorry.

If we are going to try again,

I need to stop speaking to you like that.

I want peace with you.

— Nonso
